Genetic Blood Disorders

🩸 Thalassemia

A genetic blood disorder where the body produces insufficient hemoglobin.

Symptoms

  • Fatigue
  • Pale Skin
  • Weakness
  • Delayed Growth

Treatment

  • Blood Transfusion
  • Iron Chelation
  • Bone Marrow Transplant

🧬 Sickle Cell Anemia

An inherited disorder where red blood cells become sickle shaped.

Symptoms

  • Joint Pain
  • Fatigue
  • Swelling
  • Breathing Problems

Treatment

  • Pain Management
  • Hydroxyurea Therapy
  • Blood Transfusion
